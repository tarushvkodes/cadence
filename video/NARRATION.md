## Read-through

**Cadence**

This is Cadence. We built it to help you plan your week around how you actually feel.

**The idea**

Here's the idea. Your phase doesn't change what you're capable of, but how you feel can change, and that's different for everyone. So, Cadence plans around what you log, instead of a guess from the calendar.

**Step 1: Set up**

Step one is setup. You tell it when your last period started, how long your cycle usually is, and how regular it is. Then, it makes four promises. Your data stays on your computer, the AI is only suggesting, it's not medical advice, and the dates are estimates. So, don't use it for birth control. We'll start with the sample week here. It's a busy student week with the midterm on Thursday.

**Step 2: Read your day**

Step two is Today. The dial shows you where you probably are, day 25 of 28. It gives a range instead of a date. Your next period is probably November 18th to the 22nd, and it says how sure it is. On the right is the 10-second check-in. Energy, mood, and anything else you notice. That's what our app plans around.

**Step 3: Ask the Guide**

Step three is the AI. We open the Guide and ask it, "Plan my week around my cycle." The Guide is an agent. That means the model, instead of just answering in one shot, it decides what it needs, calls tools, reads the results, and it repeats. And then, finally, it proposes. Here, it checks your cycle, it reads your calendar, habits, and check-ins, and it drafts changes. A tool checks every draft against your calendar, and when one doesn't fit, the model fixes it. And it ends with five suggestions, each explained with your own check-ins.

**Step 4: You decide**

Step four, you decide. Each suggestion shows what changes and why. We approve the ones we like and decline the ones we don't. Change your mind? Undo puts it right back. On the Plan, anything still waiting shows up as a dashed ghost block, and nothing changes until you approve it.

**Step 5: A rough morning**

Step five, a rough morning. Thursday, period day one, midterm tonight. We log it in ten seconds. Low energy, flat mood, cramps. We ask the Guide what to change, and it only touches today, and the midterm stays put.

**Step 6: Know the limits**

Step six is knowing its limits. We say our cramps are bad enough to miss class. It doesn't diagnose, it says clearly this is worth seeing a clinician and when to go to urgent care. And then it offers to turn our check-ins into notes for the appointment.

**Step 7: See inside**

Step seven, you can see inside. Our Learn page labels every claim by how strong the evidence is, with its source. And Settings shows the exact instructions the AI follows, word for word.

**Remember**

So in closing, there's three things to remember. Cadence plans around how you feel, not your phase. The AI suggests and you decide, and it knows when to send you to a real clinician. Our code is fully open source. And in the rest of our presentation, there's more detail about the different resources we're using for our platform. And you can always check the provenance of our claims. So thank you for listening. And that's our solution. Cadence.
